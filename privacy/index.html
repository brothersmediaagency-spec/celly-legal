What Celly is

Celly is a skin and wellness application. It offers skin analysis from a photo you take, scanning of foods and products, a diary, personalised suggestions, multi-day regeneration cycles and environmental context such as local weather.

Celly is not a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing it shows you is medical advice. Scores, readings and suggestions are estimates produced by software for general wellness purposes. If something about your skin concerns you, speak to a doctor or dermatologist.

Your Celly account

  • When you start using Celly, the app may automatically create an anonymous account with our backend provider, Supabase. It has no name, email or password attached — it exists so your data has an owner.
  • You can optionally sign in with Apple or with Google, which links a permanent identity to that same account so you can recover your data. This is optional; Celly works without it.
  • The Supabase user ID (UID) is the identifier Celly uses for your account throughout the service.
  • We never receive or store your Apple or Google password. Those providers verify you and hand Celly a token; the password never passes through the app or our systems.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address, which is stored with your account. When you sign in with Apple you may choose Apple's private relay address instead of your real one. Celly asks Apple only for your email address. When you sign in with Google, our authentication provider also records the basic profile details Google includes in its sign-in token, such as the name and profile-picture link of that Google account.
  • You can sign out from the Profile screen. Signing out does not delete your account or its data.

Profile information you give us

During onboarding, and whenever you edit your profile, you can provide:

  • a name (optional — it is only used to address you in the app)
  • age group
  • skin type
  • skin concerns
  • how your skin reacts to certain foods
  • dietary requirements and preferences
  • your main priority

This information is stored on your device and in your account on our backend, so it survives reinstalling the app and follows you if you sign in on another device. It is used to personalise what Celly shows you and nothing else.

Your cosmetic skin profile

What Celly holds about your skin is a cosmetic profile: the skin type you selected, the visible characteristics you chose from a fixed list — such as visible blemishes and marks, visible redness and uneven tone, enlarged pores, dryness or the appearance of fine lines — the change in appearance you say you notice most often, your dietary preferences, and your stated cosmetic goal.

These are descriptions of how skin looks, not statements about a medical condition. Celly does not ask you whether you have a diagnosed condition, does not name diseases as answer options, and does not tell you that you have one. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor or infer any illness, and nothing it shows you is a medical assessment.

You choose whether to provide any of it. Every field is something you type or tap yourself, and Celly does not derive it from anywhere else. We use it only to operate the features you are using — personalising suggestions, building your plan, showing your diary and tracking a cycle you started. We do not sell this information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

If you would rather Celly did not hold it, you can delete your account from inside the app (see Deleting your account below), which removes it from our backend.

Face scanning

  • Face analysis runs on your device, using Google ML Kit's on-device face detection. The analysis does not require an internet connection to inspect the image.
  • Your face images are never uploaded to Celly or to our backend. No photograph of you is transmitted to us or stored on our servers.
  • Celly does not perform biometric identification. It does not build a faceprint, it cannot recognise you from a face, and it does not attempt to match you against anyone else.
  • Scan images, progress photos you take during a cycle and any profile photo you choose may be stored locally, inside the app's own private storage area on your device, so you can compare results over time. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Your face-scan history — the estimated readings each scan produced — is kept on your device. Our backend records only whether you have completed a face scan, and diary entries may record the estimated skin readings shown before and after an entry.
  • Temporary frames captured while the camera guides you through a scan are written to the app's temporary storage and removed when they are no longer needed.

Diagnostics sent by Google ML Kit

The Google ML Kit components that perform on-device face detection and text recognition, together with the GoogleDataTransport library they rely on, may send diagnostic, performance and usage information to Google about how those components are running. According to Google's own declarations for these components, this information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

This telemetry is produced by Google's software inside the app. It does not contain your photographs, your profile or your diary.

Food and product scanning

  • Text recognition on labels runs on your device using Google ML Kit.
  • When you scan a barcode, that barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to look up the product.
  • No Celly account identifier is sent with that lookup. Open Food Facts receives a product code and nothing that identifies you.

Product analysis

To describe a scanned product, Celly sends the product's own details — its barcode and composition, such as name, brand, category, ingredients, nutrition values and on-pack claims — together with the language of the app, to Celly's own backend. The request is made from your signed-in Celly session.

  • If that product has not been analysed before, our backend sends those product details only to OpenAI, which generates a general description of the product's composition. OpenAI does not receive your account ID, your email, your profile, your skin information, your diary or any photograph.
  • The resulting product description is stored in our backend and reused for anyone who scans the same product. It describes the product, not you, and is not linked to your account.
  • The personalised score you see — how a product fits your profile — is calculated on your device from that product description and your profile.

Location and weather

If you allow it, Celly uses your location to show weather conditions relevant to your skin, such as UV index, humidity and temperature.

  • On iOS, Celly requests reduced (approximate) location accuracy by default. It does not ask for precise location.
  • Celly uses your approximate location only while the app is in use. It does not use background location.
  • Before sending anything, Celly rounds your coordinates to two decimal places. What leaves your device is therefore coarse location — roughly the area you are in, not where you are.
  • Those coarse coordinates are sent to Celly's own backend. Your device does not contact the weather provider directly. Our backend then requests environmental information — such as temperature, humidity and UV/weather conditions — from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute).
  • The request to MET Norway carries no account identifier, and our backend does not associate your coordinates with your account.
  • Celly does not store any location history. Your coordinates are not saved to our database. To avoid repeating identical requests, our backend may briefly keep the forecast for a rounded area in temporary memory, without any link to who asked for it.
  • To display the name of your area, Celly asks Apple's system geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. This runs through iOS system services, which means Apple's systems may receive location data under Apple's own privacy terms.
  • You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. The weather card simply disappears.

Diary and regeneration cycles

Entries you create — scanned products with their result, and your progress through a regeneration cycle — are stored on your device and also saved to your account on our backend, so they are not lost if you reinstall or change device. They are associated with your Celly account ID and are readable only by that account.

Subscriptions

  • Celly Premium is sold through the Apple App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android. The store handles the payment. We never see your payment card, bank details or billing address.
  • We use RevenueCat to know whether your subscription or free trial is active, renewed, cancelled or refunded, so the app can unlock the right features. On Android, purchases are also started through RevenueCat's software, which hands them to Google Play.
  • The identifier RevenueCat holds for you is your Supabase UID — the same Celly account ID described above — together with your purchase, trial and entitlement status and the technical store information needed to verify purchases.
  • Deleting your Celly account does not cancel your subscription. Apple or Google bills you, so only the store can stop the billing. See Deleting your account.

Analytics and diagnostics

Celly records a small number of first-party product events — for example that a scan happened, or that a screen was opened — and stores them in our own backend, associated with your Celly account ID. The event contents are restricted by design: names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, coordinates, images and tokens are rejected before an event can be written.

To be explicit about what Celly does not use:

  • no Firebase Analytics
  • no Firebase Crashlytics
  • no Mixpanel
  • no Sentry
  • no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • no cross-app or cross-site advertising tracking, and no advertising networks

The separate diagnostic telemetry produced by Google ML Kit is described above.

Technical logs

Like any online service, our backend provider and the other providers listed below automatically record technical information when the app connects to them — such as the IP address of the connection, the time of the request, the address requested and whether it succeeded. These logs are used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. They are kept for the period set by each provider's own log retention and are not used to build a profile of you.

App updates

Celly uses Shorebird to deliver small corrections to the app without a full store update. When the app starts, it may ask Shorebird's servers whether a correction is available for the installed version. That request contains technical information — such as the app version, the device platform and processor type, and a random identifier generated by the update component — and, like any connection, your IP address. It does not contain your Celly account ID, your profile or your diary.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. Celly does not currently use a remote push notification service for these reminders, which means no message content travels through a push server for them. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Artificial intelligence

Celly uses an external AI provider, OpenAI, only for the product analysis described above, and only with product details. Your profile, skin information, diary, photographs and answers are not sent to OpenAI or to any other external generative-AI provider.

Celly's personalised AI compatibility feature, which would use your profile, is disabled and is not active in the published app. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the feature is switched on.

Why we are allowed to process your information

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — creating and maintaining your account, storing your profile, diary and cycles, delivering the features you asked for, and unlocking a subscription you bought.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service working and secure, delivering app corrections, keeping technical logs, and understanding in aggregate how features are used so we can improve them. You can object to this at any time using the contact address above.
  • Your own decision to provide it — the cosmetic skin profile described above exists only because you chose to enter it, and we use it solely to operate the features you are using. If you would prefer us not to hold it, delete your account and it is removed.

Celly does not collect special-category data as defined by Article 9 of the GDPR, and therefore does not ask for the separate explicit consent that such data would require. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and consent will be requested before any such information is collected.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your Celly account exists. When you delete your account, the associated data is removed from our backend as part of that process. We do not apply a fixed retention period beyond that, and we are not stating one here that we have not implemented.

Product descriptions generated for a product are not personal data and are kept so they can be reused. Providers listed above keep their own records under their own retention rules — for example, Apple and Google retain transaction records for their own accounting and legal obligations, independently of us, and RevenueCat keeps the purchase history linked to your former account ID until we ask it to delete it.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account.

After you confirm, the request is sent to our backend, which deletes your profile, diary entries, regeneration cycles, product events and the other records held against your account, and then deletes the account itself, including the sign-in email linked to it. Only once the server confirms the deletion does the app clear the copy held on your device. If the server cannot complete the deletion, nothing is removed and the app tells you so, so you can try again.

Deleting your account in the app does not automatically remove the purchase record RevenueCat holds for your former account ID. If you want that removed too, write to cellyskinfood@gmail.com and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it.

Deleting your Celly account does not cancel your subscription. The store charges you, and only the store can stop it. On iPhone, cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions or through the App Store. On Android, cancel in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. If you delete your Celly account without cancelling, the store will keep billing you.

Resetting the app is not the same thing

Celly also offers Reset App. That clears Celly on the phone in front of you — your local skin history, scans and answers — and nothing else. Your Celly account and the data saved to it stay exactly where they are, and signing in again brings them back. Use Delete account if you want the account and its data gone.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict how we process it
  • receive your data in a portable, machine-readable form
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting what happened before you withdrew it

To exercise any of these, write to cellyskinfood@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request comes from you. Erasure is also available directly in the app, as described above.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly you can lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Children

Celly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will delete it.
